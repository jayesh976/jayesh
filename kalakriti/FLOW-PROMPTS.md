# Kalakriti: Google Flow shot list

The hero video on the site is the one you made in Google Flow (`Machine_printing_design_on_cup`).
The design cards currently use drawn mug illustrations. Make these shots in Google Flow,
download them, and send them back. They will replace the drawings one by one.

Use the same look for every shot so the site feels like one place:
soft morning window light with leaf-shaped shadows on a warm ivory wall,
a clean white and steel cup-printing machine, glossy white ceramic, shallow depth of field.
Add "no text, no logos, no watermark" to every prompt.
