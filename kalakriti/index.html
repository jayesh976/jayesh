Questions

Good to know.

Something else on your mind? Ask in the order form below.

Can I send my own photo or logo?

Yes. A clear photo, or a logo as PNG or PDF, works best. If a photo is too blurry to print well, we tell you before printing.

Will I see the design before you print?

Yes. We send you a preview on WhatsApp. We only print after you say yes.

How long does it take?

Most orders are printed in 2 to 3 days. Delivery time depends on your city.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can order just one cup. Prices drop when you order 12 or more.

How do I wash my printed cup?

Gentle hand washing keeps the colours bright for the longest time. Avoid scrubbing the print with steel wool.
