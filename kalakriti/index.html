Why it burns better

Strong scent. Even burn. Clean flame.

  • A scent you can actually smell.

    We blend generous fragrance into the wax and let each candle cure for two weeks, so the scent reaches every corner of the room.

  • No hole down the middle.

    Each wick is sized to the jar. Let the first burn melt all the way to the edge and it burns evenly to the end.

  • No black soot.

    Clean soy wax and cotton wicks. Trim the wick to half a centimetre before each light.

Questions

Before you order.

Is the scent strong?

Yes. One candle fills a bedroom or a small living room within about 20 minutes. Burn it for two to three hours at a time.

How long does one candle burn?

About 40 hours, if you trim the wick and let each burn melt the top evenly.

How do I clean the jar for reuse?

When about a centimetre of wax is left, stop burning. Put the jar in the freezer for an hour, pop out the wax, then wash it with warm soapy water.
